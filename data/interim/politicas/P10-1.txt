# BrainCo FocusCalm Digital Application Privacy Policy Notice
Version: 2.0 dated September 7, 2025
BrainCo develops applications and devices that collect data to improve learning processes and/or train attentive relaxation abilities. Our products create a personalized experience by using certain personal data to provide analysis and insights directly to the individual customer.
The FocusCalm device collects EEG and IMU signals during use. The FocusCalm application processes these raw signals locally on your device in the FocusCalm mobile app to generate several scores that reflect your meditation state. Neither EEG nor IMU raw data is transmitted to BrainCo servers. Only the computed Meditation Scores and Training History are transmitted, solely for the purpose of helping you visualize and evaluate your meditation training. The computed scores do not contain raw EEG or IMU signals and cannot be used to reconstruct the original biometric signals.
Protecting your privacy and securing your data is a top priority. This Privacy Notice provides information on how the FocusCalm application processes data and is organized into eight sections:

# 1. Purpose of Data Processing
The FocusCalm application uses data retrieved through the BrainCo headband to calculate the levels of calmness an individual has to help improve their ability to concentrate, increase productivity and enhance skills that aid relaxation.
# 2. How We Get Data
Data is received from the headband (EEG signals processed locally on the device) and from data that is entered into the application by the user.
When the application is used, limited non-biometric data is retrieved from our servers to provide historical data and to make sure that a user’s account is accessed in a secure manner.

Direct
We collect information about the individual using the application such as a nickname the user assigns themselves, gender, date of birth, and email address. # 4. Legal Basis for Data Processing
Data is processed through the FocusCalm application for clients as outlined in the FocusCalm terms of service. By using the FocusCalm application, the user is agreeing to the processing of their personal data by BrainCo on their behalf in order for the user to interact with the application.
BrainCo will not process data for anyone under the age of 13 years of age (or higher threshold required by local law).
BrainCo will not process data on behalf of an employer such that any data sharing agreement would allow for said employer to gain access to specific employee data.
# 5. Use of Data After Processing
All the data collected and processed, with the exception of EEG data, is stored on BrainCo’s servers.
BrainCo does not sell data to any individual or entity.
Non-biometric personal data may be retained as long as the account is active or as legally required, but not longer than necessary.
# 6. Rights of the Individual
Right to Know: BrainCo will keep you informed of any changes to this privacy notice, and you may reach out to use with questions regarding how your data is collected, used, and stored. Please contact us as outlined in number seven below.
Right to File Complaint: If you have any concerns about how your data is being processed by our website or questions about your right to restrict processing (where applicable), please contact us as outlined in number seven below.
Right to Access: You have a right to access the information that BrainCo stores through our website. Most of this information can be viewed by logging into the application itself. Should you require additional information about what BrainCo is storing, please contact us as outlined in number seven below.
Right to Data Portability: Users of our website have a right to request any data that is directly connected to them and generated through the application to be provided when requested. Please contact us as outlined in number seven below.
Right to Rectify: If the user of our website feels that any data that is stored is incorrect and they cannot make corrections on their own, please contact us as outlined in number seven below.
Right to be Forgotten: Should a user of our website no longer wish to have data previously submitted through the application to be stored by BrainCo, please contact us as outlined in number seven below.
