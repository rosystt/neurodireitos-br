# Privacy policy
Last updated: June 10, 2025
NextSense offers products and services focused on sleep and wellness, including NextSense Smartbuds and the NextSense mobile application.
This Privacy Policy describes how we handle personal information that we collect through Smartbuds, our mobile app, website, social media pages, online communications, and any other sites or services that link to this Privacy Policy (collectively, the "Services").
Our Privacy Principles for Brainwave Data
NextSense Smartbuds use brainwave data to improve your sleep and mental wellbeing. Here are the privacy principles that guide our use of that information:
We collect your brainwave data only with your consent.
We process your brainwave data only to help you with your sleep, meditation, and focus. The NextSense app uses algorithms to derive metrics from your brainwave data. We do not use your brainwave data to make any undisclosed inferences about you, such as your emotional or psychological state.
By default, we process your brainwave data locally on your device, without sending it to our cloud.
You may choose to let us process your brainwave data in our cloud. Users may choose to share their raw brainwave data to improve the product or contribute to research. This data is only ever sent to the cloud with explicit consent.
We do not sell your brainwave data or share it with our marketing or advertising partners. We also do not share your brainwave data with other third parties for their own purposes, except at your direction – for example, if you choose to participate in a clinical trial or other study.
You may ask us at any time to delete the information we maintain about you. This Privacy Policy explains how to submit a request.

We collect the following information generated by the Smartbuds’ sensors:

Motion data collected by the Smartbuds’ motion sensors (such as your movement during sleep).
Sleep data and sleep metrics derived from Smartbuds data (such as sleep stages and time spent asleep).
We never share the information we collect from Smartbuds with our marketing or advertising partners.

Bodily functions, vital signs, symptoms, or measurements.
