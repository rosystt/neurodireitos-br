# Privacy Policy
Sens.ai Privacy Policy
Last Updated: April 18, 2023
Here at Sens.ai Inc. (“Sens.ai”, “we”, or “us”), we take the privacy and security of your personal information very seriously.
We have prepared this privacy policy to explain the manner in which we collect, use and disclose personal information when you use our application (“App”), software, APIs, devices, products or services, visit our website located at https://sens.ai/ (the “Website”), or otherwise interact with us.
# OUR PRODUCTS AND SERVICES
Sens.ai offers personalized, adaptive brain training systems that are designed to enable you to change your brain state, train your brain, improve cognitive performance, and assess your brain activity.
Our headset’s sensors read your brain and heart signals while the App’s software translates these into light stimulation boosts and audiovisual cues. The neurofeedback (real-time audiovisual cues) trains your brain to learn healthy brain states. Data plays an important role in our service offering and helps us add value in three ways;
Adapt our service in real-time;
Guide future development so that we achieve our Visions of ‘A Community of Superconscious People Thriving and Doing Good in the World’; and
Advance science and neurotechnology for the future so that collectively we leave a positive legacy.

# Create an Account:
You do not have to create an account to visit or use our Website. However, to access certain services on our App and Website, you will be required to create an online account and provide personal information such as your email address and a password that you create. We use this information to create and administer your online account. We strongly recommend that you do not disclose your password to anyone. We will never ask you for your password in any unsolicited communication (such as letters, phone calls or email messages). If you become aware of any unauthorized access to or use of your account, you are required to notify us immediately.
# Use our Products and Services:
When you use our services, including our App and hardware devices, we will collect your email address, password, telephone number, and demographic information including date of birth, sex, and gender. We will ask questions and record response on topics including sleep, memory, meditation experience, brain fog, attention, word, stress, agitation, and adaption to change.
# Device-Specific Information:
When you use our products, such as our headsets, we will collect certain health or behavioural information such as your brain activity/engagement, levels of focus and distraction, response to stimuli and heart rate, which our software will analyze. We will also collect information about the position, orientation, and movement of the device while it is in operation. We use this information to provide our services to you, which includes adaptive brain training.
Location Information: With your consent, we may also collect your location information by accessing your device’s GPS coordinates so that we can personalize your experience with Sens.ai. You can opt-out at any time by turning off your location services for the App either within the App or in the settings of your mobile device.

If you wish to stop receiving push notifications from us, you can turn off push notifications for the App either within the App or in the settings of your mobile device.
