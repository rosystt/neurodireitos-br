# Privacy Policy
BrainBit Inc.
Last updated: November 11, 2026
# 1. Introduction
BrainBit Inc. (“BrainBit,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect personal information when you interact with our websites, online store, products, software applications, and services.
This Policy applies to:
• BrainBit websites, including brainbit.com, store.brainbit.com, and sdk.brainbit.com;
• BrainBit mobile and desktop applications, software, and connected services;
• Online purchases, account registrations, contact forms, and product inquiries;
• Sales, marketing, customer support, and other business communications.
# 2. Information We Collect
Depending on how you interact with BrainBit, we may collect the following information:
Contact and professional information: Name, email address, telephone number, company, job title, country, and other information you provide.
Business and communication information: Product inquiries, requests for quotations or demonstrations, correspondence, meeting details, professional interests, purchase intentions, and customer relationship history.
Purchase information: Orders, billing and shipping addresses, transaction details, payment status, and customer support requests. Payment card information may be processed directly by payment service providers.
Account and application information: Account identifiers, login information, preferences, application settings, and usage history.
Device and technical information: Device identifiers, operating system, software versions, Bluetooth connection information, IP address, diagnostic logs, and application performance data.
Biosignal and session information: Depending on the product or application, EEG, EMG, ECG, and other supported physiological signals, calculated measurements, training results, and session history.
Website usage information: Pages visited, interactions, cookies, analytics identifiers, and similar technical information.
We collect information directly from you, automatically through our websites and applications, and, where legally permitted, from business partners, referrals, or publicly available professional sources.
# 3. How We Use Your Information
We use personal information to:
• Respond to inquiries and provide requested product information;
• Arrange demonstrations, consultations, and meetings;
• Prepare quotations, process orders, and provide customer support;
• Manage prospective and existing customer relationships;
• Follow up on previous inquiries and business discussions;
• Provide information about relevant products, services, and business opportunities;
• Send newsletters, product announcements, educational materials, event invitations, and promotional offers where legally permitted;
• Operate and improve our websites, applications, devices, and services;
• Maintain accounts, process supported biosignal data, and provide application functionality;
• Analyze website performance and improve customer experience;
• Protect our systems, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.
We may use information about your previous inquiries, purchases, and professional interests to make our business communications more relevant.
We do not use sensitive biosignal or health-related information for advertising or marketing profiling without an appropriate legal basis and any required explicit consent.
# 4. Legal Bases for Processing
Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), UK GDPR, or similar laws apply, we process personal information based on one or more of the following:
Contractual necessity: To provide products, services, or information requested before entering into a contract.
Legitimate interests: To manage business relationships, respond to inquiries, maintain appropriate customer records, improve services, and conduct permitted business activities.
Consent: Where required for marketing communications, certain cookies, sensitive information, or other specific purposes.
Legal obligations: To comply with applicable laws, accounting requirements, and regulatory obligations.
Where we rely on legitimate interests, we consider your rights and reasonable expectations.
Where processing requires consent, you may withdraw it at any time.
# 5. Sales and Marketing Communications
When you contact BrainBit, submit an inquiry, or request information about our products, our representatives may use your contact details to respond and follow up regarding your request.
We may also contact existing customers and business contacts about relevant products, services, updates, and business opportunities where permitted by applicable law.
Marketing communications may include product announcements, newsletters, educational content, promotional offers, and invitations to events or demonstrations.
We send marketing communications only where we have an appropriate legal basis and comply with applicable requirements for the communication channel and recipient's location.
You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link included in those messages or contact us to update your preferences.
# SMS Communications
Where you have provided the necessary consent or other legally sufficient authorization, BrainBit may send SMS messages relating to:
• Product inquiries and requested information;
• Sales follow-ups and ongoing discussions;
• Demonstration scheduling and reminders;
• Customer support and order updates;
• Promotional communications, where separately authorized.
• Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
You can opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP. For assistance, contact support@brainbit.com and info@brainbit.com.
Consent to receive SMS is voluntary and is not a condition of purchase.
We do not sell or share mobile numbers, SMS opt-in information, or consent records with third parties or affiliates for their independent marketing purposes. Information may be shared with service providers and telecommunications carriers as necessary to deliver messages.
# 6. Software, Applications, and Connected Devices
BrainBit applications may process personal information, device information, and physiological signals to provide their intended functionality.
Depending on the application, this may include:
• Creating and managing user accounts;
• Connecting to compatible devices via Bluetooth;
• Recording, displaying, or analyzing EEG and other biosignals;
• Providing neurofeedback, biofeedback, and training functionality;
• Saving session results, settings, and progress history;
• Collecting technical information necessary for diagnostics and application improvement.
Some information may be processed locally on your device, while certain applications may use server-side storage or synchronization.
Applications may request Bluetooth, nearby-device, or location permissions where required for device discovery and connectivity. Such permissions do not necessarily mean that physical location information is collected or stored.
Where applicable, product-specific notices provide additional details about data collection, storage, third-party services, and account deletion.
# 7. Cookies and Analytics
Our websites may use cookies and similar technologies for essential functionality, security, analytics, user preferences, and marketing purposes.
Some technologies are necessary for the operation of our websites. Others, including certain analytics and advertising technologies, may require your consent.
Where required, we provide options to manage non-essential cookies and tracking preferences.
You may also control cookies through your browser settings.
# 8. Sharing Personal Information
We may share personal information with trusted service providers that support our operations, including:
• Website hosting and technical infrastructure;
• Customer relationship management and business communications;
• Email and SMS delivery;
• Payment processing, e-commerce, and shipping;
• Analytics, security, and customer support.
We may also share relevant information with authorized distributors or business partners when necessary to respond to your request, fulfill an order, or provide a requested service.
We require appropriate protections when third parties process personal information on our behalf.
We may disclose information when required by law, to protect our legal rights, or in connection with a corporate transaction.
We do not sell customer contact lists or sensitive biosignal information to third parties for their independent marketing purposes.
Certain advertising technologies may constitute a “sale,” “sharing,” or “targeted advertising” under applicable privacy laws. Where relevant, we provide the required disclosures and opt-out options.
# 9. International Data Transfers
BrainBit operates internationally. Personal information may be processed in the United States or other countries where we or our service providers operate.
Where required by applicable law, we use appropriate safeguards for international data transfers, including recognized contractual or other lawful transfer mechanisms.
# 10. Data Retention and Security
We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, maintain legitimate business relationships, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.
Retention periods depend on the type of information, the relevant service, and applicable legal requirements.
We periodically review the information we retain and delete or anonymize data when it is no longer needed, subject to lawful retention requirements.
We may retain limited information necessary to honor unsubscribe requests and prevent unwanted communications.
We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, loss, or misuse. However, no electronic system can guarantee absolute security.
# 11. Your Privacy Rights
Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to:
• Access personal information we hold about you;
• Request correction or deletion of your information;
• Request restriction of certain processing activities;
• Object to processing based on legitimate interests;
• Object to direct marketing at any time;
• Withdraw consent;
• Request data portability;
• Opt out of certain sales, sharing, or targeted advertising activities;
• Lodge a complaint with a relevant data protection authority.
To exercise your rights, contact support@brainbit.com and info@brainbit.com.
We may request reasonable verification of your identity before processing certain requests.
We will respond in accordance with applicable legal requirements.
# 12. Children's Privacy
Our corporate website, online store, and general business services are not directed to children under 13.
We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 through these services without any required parental authorization.
Certain BrainBit technologies may be used in research, educational, or supervised environments involving minors. Additional privacy and consent requirements may apply in those circumstances.
If you believe personal information about a child has been collected inappropriately, please contact us.
# 13. Third-Party Services
Our websites and applications may contain links to third-party services or integrate technologies provided by other organizations.
These services may have their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage you to review them when using the relevant services.
# 14. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our products, services, business practices, or applicable laws.
The latest version will be available on our website with an updated effective date.
Where required by law, we will provide additional notice or obtain renewed consent for material changes.
