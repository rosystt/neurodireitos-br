# How is my brain data being used?
Only to improve your sleep and deliver a seamless experience. The NextSense app processes data on your device to power features like sleep tracking and brain-responsive audio. We don’t send brain data to the cloud unless you opt in. If you choose to do so, the data is anonymized and stored securely.
# Can Smartbuds read my thoughts?
No. EEG measures the brain’s electrical activity, which can be used to determine brain states, but doesn’t capture or interpret thoughts.
# What if I change my mind?
You can always change your sharing preferences or choose to delete your data entirely. We respond to all privacy requests within 2 weeks.
